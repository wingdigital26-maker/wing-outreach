Turnover Radar on Richardson and Rockwall

Does a re-roof within about a block in the last 90 days predict the next one, in cities and years the Radar was never fitted on? Permits: the cities' own permit reports (Richardson 2014 on, Rockwall 2016 on), placed on homes by address. Same rule as the Collin back-test: 30-day delay before a permit counts, homes with their own re-roof in the last 5 years left out. Built 2026-10-26 by scripts/permits/turnover_backtest_cities.py.
