Contractor batches. 9,983 of 25,995 permits had 2 or more others within 100 m filed within 3 days. That also happens after every storm, so it is a generous cut. Dropping them from the neighbors side barely moves the result, so batch jobs are not what drives the lift.

McKinney only. Using only McKinney's own permit layer, the lift is smaller (about 3x for 1 nearby, 9x for 2 to 3) and the counts are small. Its permits carry only a month, not a day, and the county list (which has McKinney permits with exact days) is left out here, so timing is fuzzier. The direction holds; the size is less certain in McKinney alone.

Read honestly

Most of the lift is "this pocket is being re-roofed right now" (a storm and a wave of approved claims), not one neighbor copying another: inside the same 1 km square in the same month the lift is only about 1.3 to 1.4 times (strategy test, docs/strategy-1023/BIG-BETS.md). For picking doors that is the point: it is the freshest public sign of where claims are being paid. A permit means that homeowner already signed; the value is the homes next to it. Batch jobs by one contractor can inflate a cluster. Plano, Richardson and Rockwall have no public permit search beyond the county list, so waves there are undercounted.
