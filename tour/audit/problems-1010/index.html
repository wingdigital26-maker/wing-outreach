Roof Command, Jackson: UI problems before the Chris pitch

Audit of 2026-10-10 evening on branch ui/jackson-dfw at :4886, desktop 1440x900 and phone 390x844, headless Chromium with swiftshader. Ranked by impact on Chris's first impression. Locked looks (Today map B, CRM glass deck A, text + email only) are not questioned here.

Three things that would most raise the billion-dollar feel, inside the picked looks

  1. Instant, personal Today. Paint "Good evening, Chris" and the first house in under a second from cached data, no "Loading" pills, no "Good morning, ." skeleton. Speed is the whole feel of a premium product.
  2. The house as the hero. Roof render on a ground plane with a shadow and north mark, owner written "Noelle Appel", real traced contact fields (or an honest "not traced yet"), and the same counts on Today, Inbox and Pipeline. No 555 numbers, no "None".
  3. Nimbus speaks for Jackson. Remove every "sample tour" line, open Ask empty with three real questions, and give the orb one docked position that never covers a button on the phone.

Measured load times (Today was ~14 s before tonight)

Timing run did not finish in the time box; see log.txt for the contended numbers.

Today, Text dialog, CRM home

1440 + 390

Every homeowner shows a fake phone number, "(972) 555-0100 (sample phone)", on the hero card, in the text dialog and on the CRM contact tab, so the first thing Chris reads is placeholder data.
