What could be wrong

  • Roof plan (4,199 sq ft) is drawn from the LiDAR roof returns inside the traced outline (4,413 sq ft); the night recompute's trimmed outline gives 3,964 sq ft. A low porch or patio cover under 2.5 m is not counted.
  • About 479 sq ft of tree canopy sits on the roof edge; if roof is hidden under it, add up to 5.7 squares.
  • Trees: 16% of the returns over the roof were canopy and dropped; facets under trees are filled in from their neighbours.
  • Point density 7.5 returns/m2 (2018 flight): planes smaller than about 4 m2 (40 sq ft), such as small dormers, crickets and bump-outs, are missed or merged.
  • LiDAR is from 2018: any reroof, addition or patio cover since then is not in it.
  • Edges are read from where the fitted planes meet on a 0.25 m grid: each ridge/hip/valley length can be off by about 1 to 3 ft; eaves and rakes follow the LiDAR roof edge, squared to the house axis.
  • Step and wall flashing are inferred from height jumps between roof levels (lower roof against a second-story wall); chimneys and skylights are not detected.
  • Overhang: the outline is the roof edge seen from above; no extra overhang is added.
