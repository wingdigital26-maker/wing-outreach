A quick tour of Roof Command

Today and the Daily 50

Your home screen. The 50 best homes for today, each with the key facts (roof age, last storm, nearby re-roofs) and where each fact came from. Tap a home to call or text the owner. Owner names show as initials until you reach out.

Storms and the morning-after alert

A storm timeline for {{area}}: hail size, wind, which streets were inside the path. After a storm, {{ownerOr}} gets a morning alert by text or email with the hit streets and a ready list. Past customers in the path come first.

Turnover Radar

Shows streets where neighbors are re-roofing right now, from city permit records. When several homes on a street replace their roofs, the neighbors often follow. Homes that were just re-roofed drop off the list.

The home record and the LiDAR roof estimate

Every home has one page: year built, permits, storm history, notes and every call or text. The roof size is measured from public LiDAR laser scans of the area, so you get squares and pitch before you drive out. It is an estimate, usually within about 10%. Measure before you quote.

Proof Page

A clean one-page summary a rep can send a homeowner: the storm that hit their street, what to look for, and how to book a free inspection with {{name}}. It carries your name and number, not ours.

CRM

Every lead moves through simple stages: new, contacted, inspection booked, inspected, won or lost. Every call, text and note is logged on the home, so anyone on your team can pick up where someone else left off.

Past customers

Your past-customer list lives here. When a storm crosses a past customer's street, they are flagged first, and you approve a friendly check-in. Past customers are your warmest leads and the easiest yes.

SEO tab

How {{name}} shows up on Google: rankings for your main searches in your cities, the posts and pages we published, and Google Business activity. So you can see the website work, not just hear about it.
