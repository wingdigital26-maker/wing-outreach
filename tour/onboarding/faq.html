FAQ and ground rules

The rules, in plain words

These keep {{name}} out of trouble and keep homeowners happy to hear from you. They apply to everyone: your team and Wing.

The six ground rules

  • A person taps send on every cold message. No robo-texts or auto-emails to homeowners who have not asked to hear from you.
  • Only inside the hours. Calls {{callHours}}. Texts {{textHours}}. The app blocks sends outside them.
  • Stop means stop. Anyone who says stop, no, or take me off is marked Do not contact on the spot, for good, on every channel.
  • Roof sizes are estimates. LiDAR measurements, usually within about 10%. Measure on site before you quote.
  • No insurance or deductible promises. Never promise a payout, never offer to cover or waive a deductible, never say you handle the claim.
  • Your name on every message. Every call and text says it is {{name}}, with a real callback number.

Questions roofers ask

Do you send texts to homeowners for us automatically?

Only to people who asked for them: homeowners who signed up on your "Was my house hit?" page, and past customers who said it is OK to contact them. For everyone else, the app writes the text and a person on your team (or Wing, if agreed) reads it and taps send. That is the law for cold texts and it also gets better replies.

What are the texting and calling hours?

Calls: {{callHours}}. Texts: {{textHours}}, unless you ask for something narrower. We keep to these hours for everyone, past customers included.

What happens when someone says stop?

Reply STOP to a text, or say "do not call" on the phone, and the home is marked Do not contact right away. It stays off every list, for every rep, for good. We also scrub every list against {{noCall}} before anyone calls.

How accurate are the roof sizes?

They come from public LiDAR laser scans of the ground and buildings. Most roofs land within about 10% of a hand measurement. Some homes have older scans or tree cover and show "needs work". Use it to plan the visit and rough the job size. Always measure before you put a number on paper.

Can we talk about insurance at all?

You can say a storm crossed their street and that a free inspection will show whether there is damage worth a claim. You cannot promise insurance will pay, quote what it will pay, offer to cover the deductible, or say you will handle the claim for them. That is set by {{deductibleLaw}}. The homeowner always pays their own deductible.

Where do the homeowner phone numbers come from?

A phone lookup service on an account in your company's name. Numbers are checked against the do-not-call lists before they reach a rep. We never share your data with another roofer.

Why do owner names show as initials?

Privacy. Full names show once your team reaches out to that home, so a list never floats around with everyone's names on it.

Who owns the data?

Your past customers, notes, calls and booked jobs are yours.

What does Wing need from us each week?

Log outcomes on every call, run the booked inspections, and join a 15-minute call. That is it.

What counts as a shown inspection?

The homeowner was there (or gave access) and your team looked at the roof. Booked but cancelled does not count. Shown inspections are the main number in your monthly report.

Do you ever mail flyers or postcards?

No. Roof Command uses calls, texts a person sends, opt-in alerts, email to past customers, and your website and Google profile.
