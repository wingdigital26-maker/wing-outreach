Your first day on Roof Command

One page. Follow it top to bottom. You are calling for {{name}}, and every conversation aims at one thing: a free roof inspection.

  1. Open Today. Log in on your phone. Today shows your Daily 50: the homes most worth reaching today, best first. Work from the top.
  2. Read the card before you reach out. Roof age, last storm, nearby re-roofs. Each fact shows where it came from. Pick one fact to open with ("a hailstorm crossed your street on the 14th").
  3. Tap to call or tap to text. The app opens the call or a ready text with the owner's number filled in. You read it, you tap send. Nothing goes to a new homeowner without a person tapping send. Calls only {{callHours}}.
  4. Say who you are, first. "Hi, this is [your name] with {{name}}. I'm calling because [the storm / your neighbors' roofs]. We're offering free roof inspections on your street." Book the inspection. No prices, no contracts on the call.
  5. Log the outcome right away. Tap one: no answer, left message, talked, not interested, booked, do not contact. Add a one-line note. It takes five seconds and it is how the list gets smarter tomorrow.
  6. Book the inspection. Set the date and time on the home record and move it to Inspection booked. Tell the homeowner who is coming and when. If they want to see proof first, send the Proof Page from the home record.
  7. Measure before you quote. The roof size on the home record is a LiDAR estimate, usually within about 10%. Great for planning. On site, measure and confirm before any number goes on paper.

Always

  • Give your name and {{name}} at the start.
  • "Stop", "no" or "take me off": tap Do not contact. Done for good.
  • Max 3 calls to a home in a day.
  • Log every call and text.

Never

  • Promise an insurance payout or say "your insurance will cover it".
  • Offer to cover, waive or rebate a deductible.
  • Say you handle or negotiate the claim for them.
  • Quote an exact price from the app's roof size.
